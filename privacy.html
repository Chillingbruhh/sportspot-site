Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Sportspot helps people find sports courts, see how busy they are, and organise pickup games and tournaments. This policy explains what information we collect, why, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and your rights. We’ve tried to write it in plain English.

The short version: we collect only what the app needs to work. We don’t sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps. We never store your date of birth or your exact location.

Who we are

What we collect and why

Technical information: the IP address and basic device details that reach our servers when the app connects Our hosting provider keeps short-term logs to run the service securely.

We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.

Our legal reasons (UK GDPR)

  • To provide the service you asked for (contract): your account, check-ins, “I’m keen”, games, activities, tournaments, ratings and stats.
  • Legitimate interests: keeping players safe (reports, blocks, the word filter, the 18+ check), preventing cheating (checking locations and results for ranked games), and keeping our systems secure. We’ve balanced these against your privacy, which is why we keep as little as possible.
  • Your consent: appearing on leaderboards by name, and sharing your location when you check in. You can withdraw these any time (leaderboard setting in the app, location in your phone’s settings).
  • Legal obligations: if the law requires us to keep or share information.

Who can see your information

  • Other players see your nickname only when you’re in the same game, tournament or activity (or have asked to join an activity: its host sees your nickname), or on leaderboards if you turned that on. Everyone else sees “Player 3” and similar. Check-ins are only ever shown as a headcount.
  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in system. Their servers may be outside the UK; where they are, the transfer is protected by safeguards approved under UK law (such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum).
  • Apple and Google handle Sign in with Apple and Sign in with Google, and provide the maps (Apple Maps on iPhone, Google Maps on Android) under their own privacy policies.
  • Expo provides the tools we use to deliver the app and, in future, send notifications.
  • Authorities, only if the law requires it.

We never sell your information.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and game history: until you delete your account.
  • Your current check-in: it expires after two and a half hours.
  • Check-in history: 90 days, then deleted automatically.
  • “I’m keen”: for the day you tap it, then deleted.
  • Activities: shown until 3 hours after they start, deleted after 30 days.
  • Reports: as long as needed to deal with them and protect players, normally no more than 12 months. When an account is deleted, its link to reports is removed.
  • Server logs: kept for a short period by our hosting provider.

Deleting your account

In the app, open You and tap Delete my account. This permanently deletes your account, nickname, check-ins, ratings, games you host (with their results) and your place in other games. Reports are kept, without the link to your account, as described above. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask us to:

  • give you a copy of your information,
  • correct it,
  • delete it,
  • restrict or object to how we use it, and
  • send it to you or another service in a common format.

Email CONTACT_EMAIL and we’ll reply within one month. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Age limit

Sportspot is only for people aged 18 and over. If we learn that someone under 18 is using it, we’ll delete their account.

Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections. Our database only lets the app use it through checked functions, so the app can’t read other players’ details directly. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and keep only what we need.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
